Thursday, Oct 22

Land, bathhouse, monja

2:15 PM · Land at Haneda

Train to Nohga Hotel Akihabara: 51 minutes to JR Akihabara (¥480), then 6 minutes on foot. Drop the bags and rest.

Evening

Koganeyu

Remodeled neighborhood bathhouse with a sauna, a big cold plunge, and a beer bar with a DJ booth for after.

11 AM to 12:30 AM¥600 + ¥550 sauna
Directions
More info
From Akihabara, the JR Sobu local three stops to Kinshicho, then 6 minutes on foot from the north exit. The bath is ¥600 for 90 minutes; the men's sauna is ¥550 more on weekdays (2 hours), and the big cold plunge is in the sauna area, so get the sauna. Tattoos are fine. Last order at the bar is midnight. Closed only on the 2nd and 4th Mondays.

Dinner

Tsukishima Monja Street

Tokyo's own griddle dish, cooked on the iron plate in your table. Over 80 monja shops on one street.

Lines around 6 PM
Directions
More info
Moheji Honten (weekdays 5 to 11 PM) takes reservations. Monkichi puts its average at ¥3,000 to ¥4,000 a head. Takenoko is the shop a Jungle Pocket comedian took over from his mom.

Friday, Oct 23

Senso-ji, Akihabara, MAPPA, Ginza

First thing · Check out, Nohga holds the bags

Leave the bags at the desk. Suehirocho on the Ginza line is 4 minutes from the hotel and runs straight to Asakusa.

Morning

Senso-ji and Azuma Bridge

Tokyo's oldest Buddhist temple, up Nakamise's 90-odd shops, then the bridge over the Sumida toward Skytree.

Main hall 6:30 AM to 5 PMLit until about 11 PM
Directions
More info
From Azuma Bridge you see Philippe Starck's golden Asahi flame on the far bank. 5 minutes from Asakusa Station.

11 AM

Super Potato

Retro games in Akihabara, back on the Ginza line.

11 AM to 8 PM
Directions

Then

Mandarake Complex

Also in Akihabara: anime and manga.

Noon to 8 PM
Directions
1:30 PM · Bags to Shiomi

Pick up the bags at Nohga. JR to Tokyo, then the Keiyo line to Shiomi. Check-in is 3 PM; if you're early the desk holds them. Then the Keiyo line back to Tokyo Station for MAPPA (the Keiyo platforms are a long walk inside the station; allow 15 minutes).

Afternoon

MAPPA EXPO

15 years of the Chainsaw Man, Attack on Titan and Jujutsu Kaisen studio. Opening day.

¥2,000 timed ticketsLast entry 5 PM
Directions
More info
At Tokyo International Forum in Yurakucho, a few stops down the JR Yamanote line. Runs Fri 10/23 to Sun 10/25, 10 AM to 6 PM; tickets through Lawson Ticket. Same-day tickets (¥2,200) are sold at the door only if timed slots are left. If it's sold out, skip it and stay in Akihabara.

Sunset

KITTE Garden

Free rooftop garden looking down on Tokyo Station's restored Marunouchi building. Sunset is 4:56 PM.

Free11 AM to 11 PM
Directions
More info
6th floor of KITTE Marunouchi, a short walk from MAPPA. It can close in bad weather.

Evening

Nissan Crossing + Ginza buildings

The Fairlady Z Version ST, Nissan cars on your drink, then Maison Hermès and Mikimoto.

10 AM to 8 PM
Directions
More info
Right outside Ginza Station exits A3 to A5. Renzo Piano's glass-block Maison Hermès and Toyo Ito's Mikimoto tower are a short walk.

Dinner

Ton Ton

Smoky skewer joint under the JR tracks since 1953. Beer to the sound of trains.

Skewers ¥210Cash
Directions
More info
Mostly offal skewers over binchotan. About ¥3,000 to ¥5,000 each. No reservations, English menu. One minute from Hibiya Station.

Late

Bar High Five, Ginza

Hidetsugu Ueno's basement bar, famous for his hand-carved ice.

Mon to Sat, 5 PM to 1 AMNo reservations
Directions
12:11 AM · Last train home

Ginza-itchome, Yurakucho Line to Shin-Kiba, then JR one stop. Into Shiomi at 12:31 AM.
