7 Petting Zoos and Animal Encounters Near Durham That Are Actually Open on Weekdays

Most Triangle farms are weekend-only or closed until spring. We found the ones that aren't.

By Katie Moorefield · March 10, 2026 · 6 min read

Here's what nobody tells you about petting zoos in the Triangle: most of them are only open on Saturdays. Maybe Sundays if you're lucky. Try to find one that'll let you feed a goat on a Tuesday afternoon and you'll burn 45 minutes on Google only to find out everywhere is either "seasonal," "by appointment only," or straight up closed until May.

We get asked about this constantly, so we finally sat down and called, clicked, and cross-referenced every farm and animal encounter within about an hour of Durham to figure out which ones are legitimately open on weekdays. This list is current as of March 2026, but hours shift seasonally so always double-check before you make the drive.

The List

Hawk Manor Falconry

Lillington, NC · ~45 min from Durham

Okay, this one isn't a petting zoo. But it might be the single coolest animal experience within an hour of Durham, and we're putting it first because not enough people know about it. Master Falconer Chip Gentry runs 2-hour sessions where you learn about the ancient sport of falconry, watch hawks and falcons fly, and yes, have a bird of prey land on your arm. They've got owls and vultures too. The whole operation is at a farm that also has goats, sheep, pigs, ducks, and chickens, so you're getting farm vibes alongside the raptors.

They do birthday parties and customize group experiences. There's an indoor facility so weather isn't an issue. And there's a Groupon floating around that knocks the price down. Reservation required.

Hours: By reservation
Cost: Starts around $50/person (check Groupon for deals)
Phone: (910) 984-5196
Ages: 6+

Haven Farm

Knightdale, NC · ~30 min from Durham

Haven Farm is one of those spots that does a lot of things. It's a wedding venue, a gift shop, a plant nursery, a snack bar, and also a petting zoo. The 40-acre property in Knightdale has a pretty, modern-farmhouse feel, and the petting zoo and nature playground are open whenever the shop is, which is Wednesday through Sunday, 10am to 6pm. The goats are the main draw. One thing to note: a recent visitor mentioned it's now $5 per person to enter the goat area, which is new. Still cheap, and the rest of the grounds are free to walk around.

Hours: Wed-Sun, 10am-6pm
Cost: ~$5/person for petting zoo
Phone: (984) 733-5517
Address: 8521 Buffaloe Rd, Knightdale

Rancho Chikiito Mobile Zoo

Based in Smithfield · They come to you

This is the option for people who don't want to drive anywhere. Rancho Chikiito is a mobile petting zoo that brings goats, chickens, bunnies, pigs, and ponies straight to your location. They set everything up, manage the animals, and clean up after. You just show up and enjoy it. They do birthday parties, neighborhood events, school visits, whatever. Durham is slightly outside their free-travel radius from Smithfield, so expect a small extra fee, but it's not crazy. If you're trying to surprise someone with a birthday petting zoo in their own backyard, this is how you do it.

Hours: Flexible, by booking
Cost: Package-based (pony rides extra)
Web: ranchochikiito.com

Historic Oak View County Park

Raleigh, NC · ~35 min from Durham

If your budget is zero dollars, this is your spot. Oak View is a Wake County-operated historic farmstead from the 1800s, and it's free to visit Monday through Saturday, 8:30am to 5pm. The goat yard has friendly Nubian and Mini-Nubian goats out from 9am to 4:30pm, and there's a pen with heritage-breed chickens. There's also a Farmer's Corner play area that's great for little kids. Is it going to blow anyone away? Probably not. But it's free, it's consistently open, and the goats are genuinely friendly. Works great as a quick stop paired with something else.

Hours: Mon-Sat, 8:30am-5pm
Cost: Free
Address: 4028 Carya Dr, Raleigh

Priority Farm

Sanford, NC · ~50 min from Durham

Priority Farm is the quiet, tucked-away option on this list. It's a 27-acre property in Sanford with 11 alpacas, mini donkeys, mini cows, an angora rabbit, chickens, and a big sweet livestock guardian dog. There are trails through the wooded part of the property and a small store selling alpaca-fiber items. The farm's mission is mentoring kids from single-parent homes, so there's a community focus that feels good to support. Visits are by appointment only, but they're responsive if you call or text.

Hours: By appointment
Cost: Contact for pricing
Phone: (919) 454-8361
Address: 1042 Buckhorn Rd, Sanford

The Pint Sized Pasture

Sanford, NC · ~45 min from Durham

Everything at The Pint Sized Pasture is miniature and it's exactly as charming as it sounds. This 6th-generation family farm keeps mini pigs, mini donkeys, mini horses, mini cows, goats, and chickens. Their public Family Farm Days are hugely popular, with yard games, bouncy houses, and corn cribs alongside the animals, but the next one isn't until March 22. The good news is they're pretty flexible about scheduling private visits if you reach out. They do work full-time jobs, so give them a day or two to respond. Call, text, or email.

Hours: By appointment (next Farm Day: 3/22)
Cost: Varies
Phone: (919) 498-4466
Email: pintsizedpasture@gmail.com

Rancho Chikiito Mobile Zoo

Wait, didn't we already list this one? Yes. We're mentioning it twice because honestly, for a birthday or special occasion, having animals show up at your house hits different. Just saying.

A Few That Didn't Make the Cut

We looked into a bunch of other spots that are either closed on weekdays, seasonal, or not quite open yet for spring 2026. A few worth keeping on your radar for weekends or later in the season:

  • Carolina Tiger Rescue (Pittsboro) has 50+ rescued big cats but is Friday through Sunday only.
  • Old Mill Farm (Durham) has a camel named Cornbread and is great when it's open, but their off-season hours are inconsistent. Call before driving out.
  • Thornfield Heritage Farm (Hillsborough) is adorable and only 15 minutes from Durham but weekends only.
  • The Feathered Farm (Zebulon) is a great interactive petting farm but closed on Wednesdays.
Bottom line: If you want the easiest, most reliable weekday petting zoo experience near Durham, Haven Farm in Knightdale and Historic Oak View in Raleigh are your safest bets since they're open without a reservation. For something more special, call Hawk Manor Falconry or The Pint Sized Pasture a few days ahead.

Hours and pricing shift seasonally. We verified everything in March 2026 but always recommend calling ahead or checking social media before you go. If we missed a spot, drop us a line.
