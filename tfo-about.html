About Us

We're a small team of parents in the Triangle who got tired of Googling "things to do with kids near me" and getting the same ten results.

Triangle Family Outings started in 2024 as a shared Google Doc between three friends who kept texting each other the same question: "Is there anything to do with kids on a Tuesday?" We'd find a great farm or nature trail or museum, tell each other about it, and then six months later nobody could remember the name.

So we started writing things down. Then we started fact-checking. Then we started calling places to verify hours because half the websites in the Triangle haven't been updated since 2019. And somewhere along the way it turned into this.

We cover Durham, Raleigh, Chapel Hill, and everywhere in between. Our focus is practical, verified information for families. We call ahead. We check seasonal hours. We note when parking is terrible. We tell you if a place is worth the drive or if you should just go to the park instead.
